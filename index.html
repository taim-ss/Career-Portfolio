02 — Interviewing & Networking

Key Realisation

Through the career fair, I learned that technical engineering skills are just the baseline. Mastering strategic communication, active networking, and cultural adaptability is essential for real momentum in Germany's industrial sector. It's not only about what you build — it's how you connect, how you read the room, and how you interpret the unspoken rules.

Being in front of a class involves psychological aspects, and also organisational aspects. — Vocational Training Manager, Rossmann

The Competitive Landscape

In Germany, the job market is very competitive. A standard toolkit of hard skills is essentially the minimum requirement — strong soft skills are the differentiator.

Language is Non-Negotiable

Every interaction confirmed that German fluency is an absolute baseline — the core operational language needed to navigate corporate administration and join training programmes.

Interview Insights

Actively listening to the Rossmann representative revealed how important adaptability and social intelligence are. Seamlessly networking with industry professionals is itself a skill.

Key Takeaway

Industry representatives emphasised that psychological awareness and effective organisational capabilities are just as vital as functional technical expertise.

05 — Career Exploration

The Catalyst: Mercedes-Benz Factory Excursion

The visit provided a deep, real-world look into modern manufacturing — not just theory, but the actual systems, people, and decisions that keep large-scale production running.

Workforce Observation: Mind vs. Hands

A clear division emerged among the factory workforce. Employees either carry out physical tasks on the production floor, or direct overarching systems from behind a desk — the analytical layer commanding the physical one.

Solidifying My Path

This contrast clarified my career motivation. I am drawn to strategic, office-oriented roles — using creativity and intellect to tackle hard logistical problems. The efficient organisational layout of the facility deepened my interest in AI integration, process modelling, and industrial engineering at the systems level.

Why This Matters

Seeing the gap between the analytical and physical layers of manufacturing makes the value of Digital Twins and automated decision systems concrete — not abstract. That's the space I want to work in.
